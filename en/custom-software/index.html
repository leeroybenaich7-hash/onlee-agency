How we work

Simple, no surprises.

You talk directly to the person building your software. No jargon, no 40-page spec.

  1. 01

    We talk

    You explain how you work today: the paper, the Excel files, the WhatsApp messages. We ask questions, lots of them.

  2. 02

    You see a first version

    Before going far, we show you clickable screens with your real products or your real customers. You tell us what works and what doesn’t.

  3. 03

    We build it together

    We move forward screen by screen. You test as we go, and nothing is set in stone until you’re happy with it.

  4. 04

    We stick around

    Updates, fixes, new ideas: your software keeps evolving with your business.

Prepare your software project on one page

  • Write down what you do every day, in order, in your own words.
  • List who will use it: you, the office, the people out in the field, your customers.
