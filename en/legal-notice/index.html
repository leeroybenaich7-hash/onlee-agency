Site traffic is measured anonymously, without cookies and without identifying visitors.

Microsoft Clarity audience measurement, with consent: only if you click “Accept” in the banner, Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation) records, in anonymised form, how pages are browsed (clicks, scrolling, movements) so we can improve the site.
